Network reconnaissance report

recon-report

A command-line tool that runs Nmap service discovery against a host you are authorized to test, rates the exposed services, and writes findings reports in HTML and Markdown.

Each report has an executive summary, the open ports per host, one card per finding with evidence and a recommendation, and a methodology section. Reports are single self-contained files.
